How the price cycle works

  1. Prices jumpRetailers push prices up, often 20c a litre or more, in a few days.
  2. Then slowly fallStations undercut each other and prices drift down over the next week or two.
  3. We email you at the bottomThe cheapest time to fill up, just before prices jump again.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no paid plans, and we never sell or share your email.

How often will you email me?

Only when your city hits the bottom: every few weeks in the east, weekly in Perth. Nothing else.

Why is there a price cycle at all?

One retailer pushes prices up, others follow, then prices drift down. The ACCC says it isn't driven by costs. Don't like it? Tell your MP.

Doesn't PetrolSpy already do this?

Sort of. PetrolSpy shows prices, but you have to check it and it has ads. We just email you when it matters.

Where does the information come from?

ACCC buying tips, checked every weekday, plus FuelWatch's next-day prices for Perth. We're not affiliated with either.

How long do I have to fill up?

In Perth, the rest of the day: the email arrives the afternoon before prices jump. Elsewhere, the sooner the better.

Why only these five cities?

They're where the ACCC tracks regular price cycles.

What do you store about me?

Just your email and city. No tracking cookies. Visits are counted anonymously.

How do I unsubscribe?

One click, from any email.
